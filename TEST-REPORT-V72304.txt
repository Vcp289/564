LuckyNumber V7.23.04 COOL SAVE INCREMENTAL PRO — 2026-08-29

ROOT CAUSE OF HOT / LONG ONE-DAY SAVE IN V7.23.03
1. hybrid-core reliableSync() always checked/advanced WF until the entire Profile covered current History.
2. Even a newest single-day Save then called LNCanonicalHistory.hydrateProfile(id,{full:true}), scanning the full 100–226 draw Profile and potentially running producer-repair rounds.
3. An automatic bootstrapOnce() started ~1.8s after launch/import and could serialize repair jobs across every Profile. On iPhone this creates sustained CPU load/heat even though History/Analysis already render correctly via Direct Source.

V7.23.04 FIX
- Normal newest-day Save is O(1)-style incremental work: at most one WF row (maxRows:1), one-row P18/P19/X3 status publish, one-row canonical ensureRows.
- Removed full canonical hydrate from newest Save path.
- Disabled automatic all-Profile bootstrap on launch/import. No automatic full repair just because the app opened.
- Historical add/edit/delete keeps serialized suffix repair because prior-only downstream rows can legitimately change.
- Durable source save remains immediate; background persistence remains deferred/coalesced.
